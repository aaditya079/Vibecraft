Weekly timetable

How it's calculated

Remaining classes = every timetable hour from now to the date (weekends & holidays removed; today's finished periods don't count).
Need for target t: max(0, ⌈t·(held+left) − attended⌉).
Irreversible: (attended+left_sem)/(held+left_sem) < 75% — even perfect attendance can't save it.
Safe skips now: ⌊attended/0.75 − held⌋.

Floor by floor

Room details & assumptions

Which room each class uses comes straight from the 10 timetables: the section's venue for regular slots, plus the rooms printed for labs, CDC, workshop and so on. Floor = the first digit of the room number (IST 20/21 = ground floor). The timetables don't list AC or seating, so those are estimates — correct them below and search uses your values. Only rooms that appear in these timetables are tracked.
